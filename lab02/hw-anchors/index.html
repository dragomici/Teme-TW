Overview

This survey collects the opinion of students from the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science about the Web Technologies labs. We want to know when you prefer to have extra sessions and which topics you would like to practise more.

The answers are used only to plan the labs. Not sure about something? Read the question about your data in the FAQ.

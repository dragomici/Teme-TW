Who can take part in the survey?

Every student of the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, from any year of study, bachelor or master. If you are ready, go directly to the survey.

Back to top

What happens with my answers?

Nothing is stored. The form has no server yet: when you press Submit, the browser only reloads the page and shows your answers in the address bar (this is what method="get" does). A real application would also check the data on the server.

Back to top

Which fields are required?

Name (at least 3 characters), email, year of study and the preferred session time. If one of them is missing, the browser stops the form and tells you what to fix. The Student ID is optional, but if you fill it in it must have exactly 6 digits. See the survey form.

Back to top

Can I fill in the form using only the keyboard?

Yes. Press Tab to move to the next field and Shift + Tab to go back. Use the arrow keys to choose a session time, Space to tick a topic and Enter to submit. The field you are on always has an orange outline.

Back to top

Can I change my answers?

Yes. Before submitting, press Reset to clear the whole form. After submitting, simply fill in the form again – the newest answers count.

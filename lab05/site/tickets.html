Tickets and prices

Early-bird prices are valid for reservations until 31 May 2027. Students show a valid student card at the entrance.

What is included

  • Entry to both stages on the days of your ticket
  • All workshops – see the workshop times
  • Free shuttle bus from Moonlake railway station
  • Camping only with the “All three days + camping” pass

How to buy

Reserve your tickets by e-mail and pay at the ticket desk when you arrive (cash or card).

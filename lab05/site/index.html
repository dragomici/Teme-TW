Jazz under the stars

For the fifth summer in a row, the Moonlake Jazz Festival brings jazz to the shore of Lake Moonlake: two stages, ten concerts, free workshops and a camping area right by the water. Bring a blanket, find a spot on the grass and enjoy the music under the stars.

Three days, three moods

Friday – Opening night

Calm piano trios while the sun goes down, then the Moonlake Big Band plays swing until midnight.

Friday programme

Saturday – Swing day

Workshops in the morning, swing dancing in the evening and a jam session late at night.

Saturday programme

Sunday – Family day

Jazz brunch on the shore, a drum workshop for children and fireworks at the closing concert.

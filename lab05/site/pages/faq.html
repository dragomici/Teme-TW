Questions and answers

Can I bring my own food?
Yes, snacks and a picnic are welcome. Please do not bring glass bottles.
What happens if it rains?
The Main stage has a roof. Concerts on the Lake stage move into the workshop tent.
Can I come with my dog?
Yes, on a lead – but dogs are not allowed in the camping area.
Can I get around in a wheelchair?
Yes. Paved paths lead from the entrance to both stages, and there are accessible toilets next to the info point.
Do children need a ticket?
Children under 12 get in free together with an adult.
